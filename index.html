Port of Poti

The Gateway to Georgia

The Port of Poti is Georgia's largest port and a critical maritime hub for the Black Sea, Caucasus, and Central Asia. We ensure your vessel is fully stocked and ready for the next leg of its journey across the Black Sea and beyond.

Our local team guarantees seamless coordination from quayside to anchorage, with deep knowledge of the port's operations and regulations.
